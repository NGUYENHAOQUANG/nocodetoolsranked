## About Tide

Tide is a business platform built for small businesses, freelancers, sole traders, and limited companies. While many people know Tide for business accounts and financial tools, the company also offers online business tools that help users create and manage their digital presence. Its Own Website Plus product is focused on helping small businesses build, host, and manage a professional website without coding. Tide says its website builder includes online hosting, free UK domain names, three company email addresses, appointment bookings, and dashboard management.

Tide positions Own Website Plus as a simple online website builder for small businesses that want to get online quickly. Instead of buying a domain, hosting, email, booking software, and payment tools from separate providers, users can manage several essentials from one Tide account. This makes the platform useful for service providers, trades, retailers, consultants, and local businesses that need a clean website without a complex setup.

The platform is especially helpful for business owners who want a website connected to real business operations. Tide's Own Website Plus supports bookings, service listings, calendar syncing, checkout, domain setup, hosting, and email. This gives users more than a simple brochure website. It can help customers book appointments, view services, and pay online when supported by the account setup.

## How it Works?

Tide Own Website Plus works by combining website creation, domain management, hosting, email, bookings, and checkout tools in one setup. Users first need to join Tide and open a free account. After that, they can access Own Website Plus through the Tide app by going to the Sell tab and selecting Memberships. Tide states that users can activate a free trial from there.

The website builder starts with layout and style selection. Users can choose from layouts designed for professional services, trade workers, or retail storefronts. They can then update typography, colors, branding, service details, and availability. Tide also allows calendar syncing with Google, iCloud, or Office 365, which is useful for appointment-based businesses.

Once the website is ready, users can publish it online and manage bookings from the dashboard. Own Website Plus also supports digital checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and debit or credit cards for eligible members with an Adyen account.

## Brand Features and Tools

Tide Own Website Plus includes tools designed to help small businesses launch and manage an online presence. The strongest features are its no-code builder, custom domain, hosting, business email, booking system, and checkout support.

**No-Code Website Builder**

Tide's website builder is made for users who do not want to write code. Business owners can choose a layout, adjust the look, add content, and publish a professional site without needing a developer. This makes it suitable for service providers, tradespeople, consultants, small retailers, and new businesses that need a fast online setup.

The builder gives users a structured starting point instead of a blank page. Layouts are designed around business types, so users can focus on updating content, branding, service details, and contact information.

**Domain and Hosting**

Tide includes web hosting and domain support with its website plans. Own Website Plus includes a free custom domain, while the free Tide Business Page uses a subdomain only. Tide's page also states that its paid website plans include unlimited products and free custom domain access.

This is useful because many small businesses do not want to manage hosting and domain settings separately. Tide keeps these core website needs inside the same business platform.

**Business Email**

Tide's own website allows users to create up to three email addresses under their registered domain. This means a business can use branded emails such as info@yourbusiness.co.uk or sales@yourbusiness.co.uk instead of relying on a personal email address. Tide also notes that each email account includes 5GB of storage by default and uses SPF, DMARC, and DKIM authentication methods.

A branded email address can help small businesses look more professional when sending quotes, confirmations, invoices, and customer updates.

**Booking Tools**

Own Website Plus includes booking features for appointment-based businesses. Customers can select appointments or group event hours from a public scheduling calendar, while the business owner can manage bookings, cancellations, and updates from the dashboard.

This is a strong feature for salons, tutors, consultants, instructors, home service providers, and other businesses that rely on scheduled appointments.

**E-commerce Checkout**

Tide's own website plus supports digital checkout for online payments. The checkout can support Apple Pay, Google Pay, and debit or credit cards for members with an Adyen account.

This gives small businesses a way to turn website visitors into paying customers without needing to connect several outside tools. However, the payment availability depends on account eligibility and payment setup.

**Analytics and Business Tools**

Tide's plan comparison shows that Own Website Plus includes advanced insights, while lower website options may have fewer analytics features.

Analytics can help website owners understand how customers interact with their online presence. For small businesses, this can support better decisions around services, booking availability, product listings, and promotions.

## How to Create a Website with Tide?

Creating a website with Tide is designed to be simple. The process starts with a Tide account and continues through the app.

**Step 1: Open a Tide Account**

Users need to join Tide first. Tide states that joining gives users access to a free business bank account and savings account, then Own Website Plus can be selected from the app.

**Step 2: Choose Own Website Plus**

After downloading the Tide app, users can go to the Sell tab, select Memberships, and activate Own Website Plus. Tide also mentions a free trial for users who want to test the tool first.

**Step 3: Pick a Layout and Style**

Users choose a persona and style based on their business type. Tide provides layouts for professional services, trade workers, and retail storefronts. Users can then adjust colors, typography, and branding.

**Step 4: Add Services, Products, or Business Details**

Next, users add the main website content. This can include business descriptions, services, rates, products, opening hours, contact details, and customer-facing information.

**Step 5: Set Availability and Bookings**

For appointment-based businesses, Own Website Plus allows users to set availability and sync calendars with Google, iCloud, or Office 365. This helps customers book available time slots directly through the website.

**Step 6: Connect Domain and Email**

Users can register a domain through Own Website and create up to three email addresses under that domain. Tide says domain registration normally takes 2 to 24 hours, and SSL certificates are issued automatically once settings are published.

**Step 7: Publish Website**

Once the content, layout, domain, email, and booking settings are ready, users can publish the website. Tide states that website settings take effect after publishing from the Website tab.

## Ecommerce

Tide Own Website Plus includes ecommerce-style features for small businesses. Users can publish a storefront, list services, set rates, manage availability, and accept digital payments when eligible. The plan comparison also shows unlimited product limits across Tide's website options, with Own Website Plus offering fully enabled checkout and advanced stock and category tools.

This makes Tide useful for businesses that sell services, appointments, or simple product offerings. It may not replace a full e-commerce platform for large stores, but it can work well for small businesses that want a practical online selling setup.

## Payment

Tide Own Website Plus supports checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and debit or credit cards for members with an Adyen account.

This payment setup helps businesses collect payments from website visitors. For service providers, this can reduce manual payment follow-ups and make booking or buying easier for customers.

## Pricing

Tide offers different website-related options depending on business needs. The Own Website Plus page lists the following UK plan costs, excluding VAT.

| Plan | Monthly Price | Key Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tide Business Page | GBP 0.00 | Subdomain, basic online presence |
| Own Website | GBP 7.99 + VAT | Custom domain, website builder, hosting |
| Own Website Plus | GBP 17.99 + VAT | Custom domain, emails, bookings, checkout, and advanced tools |

Own Website Plus is the stronger option for businesses that need bookings and checkout. The lower-cost Own Website plan may work for businesses that only need a simple website, domain, hosting, and email setup.

## The Final Verdict

Tide Own Website Plus is a practical website builder for small businesses that want more than a basic landing page. It combines a no-code website builder with hosting, a custom domain, three branded email addresses, booking tools, and checkout support.

The platform is especially useful for service businesses, local shops, freelancers, trades, consultants, and appointment-based businesses. Instead of managing several providers, users can handle the website, domain, email, bookings, and customer payments from the Tide ecosystem.
