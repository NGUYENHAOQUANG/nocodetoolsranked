## About Squarespace Website Builder

Many businesses want a professional website without hiring developers or managing separate hosting systems. Website builders simplify this process by combining design, publishing, security, and business tools within one platform.

Squarespace is an all-in-one website builder and content management system. It allows users to create business websites, online stores, blogs, portfolios, membership areas, and service pages without managing their own servers. Every paid website plan includes fully managed cloud hosting, mobile-optimized layouts, SSL security, and built-in website management tools.

The platform is particularly well known for its design-focused templates. Squarespace provides templates for industries such as photography, consulting, fashion, food, weddings, podcasts, health, fitness, and professional services. Users can customize these designs with their own colors, typography, images, navigation, and page structures.

Squarespace also supports businesses that want to sell online. All current paid website plans allow users to sell products or services, although transaction fees and advanced commerce features vary by plan. The platform can support physical products, digital downloads, subscriptions, courses, memberships, services, and donations.

Another advantage is the integration between website design and business management. Users can manage their domain, content, customer profiles, products, orders, analytics, and marketing activities from one account.

Overall, Squarespace focuses on helping users create visually polished websites while reducing the technical work involved in hosting, security, publishing, and maintenance.

## How Does It Work?

Squarespace works as a cloud-based website builder. Users create and manage their websites through a browser-based dashboard without installing website software or configuring a server.

The process begins with a free trial. Users can choose a professionally designed template or use Blueprint AI Builder to create a personalized starting design. The AI-assisted process asks questions about the website's purpose, brand, preferred style, and required pages before generating an initial structure.

After selecting a starting design, users customize the website through the visual editor. Text blocks, images, galleries, videos, forms, buttons, products, and other elements can be added to individual page sections.

Users can also adjust site-wide colors, fonts, animations, spacing, navigation, headers, and footers. This helps maintain a consistent brand design across the entire website.

Squarespace provides hosting automatically. Once the content and design are ready, users select a paid plan, connect a custom domain, and publish the website.

After launch, the dashboard allows website owners to update content, review analytics, manage products, communicate with customers, and monitor sales from one place.

## Squarespace Features and Tools

Squarespace includes tools for website design, content publishing, online selling, marketing, and business management. Its feature set is broad enough to support both simple personal websites and growing business sites.

Below are the main tools available through the Squarespace website builder.

### Website Templates

Squarespace offers hundreds of customizable templates designed for a wide range of industries and website goals. Available designs include options for portfolios, restaurants, consultants, photographers, online stores, blogs, local businesses, events, and personal brands.

Templates provide ready-made page structures and coordinated typography, colors, and visual styles. Users can replace the example content and change the appearance to match their brand.

All modern Squarespace templates are designed to work across desktop computers, smartphones, and tablets. Users can also preview and adjust how certain sections appear on different screen sizes.

### Drag-and-Drop Editor

Squarespace uses a visual drag-and-drop system known as Fluid Engine. It allows users to move, resize, and arrange page elements within customizable sections.

Users can add text, images, videos, buttons, forms, maps, galleries, menus, social links, products, and newsletter signup blocks. These elements can be repositioned without writing code.

The editor offers more layout control than rigid template systems while maintaining structured design guidelines. Users who need deeper customization can also use custom CSS and, on qualifying plans, advanced code options.

### Blueprint AI Website Builder

Squarespace Blueprint AI helps users generate a personalized website starting point. Users provide information about their business, website goals, desired pages, and preferred style.

The system can recommend a website structure, design direction, text, and visual presentation. Users can then refine every part of the generated website using the standard visual editor.

Blueprint AI can be helpful for users who do not know which template or layout to choose. However, all AI-generated text and design elements should be reviewed before publication.

### Blogging and Content Management

Squarespace includes built-in blogging tools for businesses, writers, creators, and publishers. Users can create articles, organize posts with categories and tags, schedule publication dates, add authors, and manage comments.

Blog posts can contain text, images, videos, galleries, newsletters, and other content blocks. Website owners can also place selected content behind a paywall and sell access through memberships.

These tools make Squarespace suitable for businesses that rely on educational content, company news, thought leadership, or search-driven articles.

### Portfolio Tools

Squarespace works particularly well for photographers, designers, artists, architects, and other creative professionals. Portfolio pages allow users to present projects through image galleries, videos, case studies, and individual project pages.

Visual layouts can be adjusted to create grid-based portfolios, slideshows, full-width images, or editorial-style presentations.

### Analytics and Reporting

Squarespace includes analytics tools that help users understand website performance. Reports can show traffic, visitor sources, popular content, search keywords, sales, conversion activity, and customer behavior.

Commerce users can review revenue, orders, product performance, and purchase activity. These insights help businesses identify popular pages and improve their content or marketing strategy.

Squarespace also supports connections with Google Analytics and Google Search Console for additional reporting and search performance data.

### Security and Hosting

Every Squarespace website plan includes managed cloud hosting. Users do not need to purchase hosting separately or maintain their own server.

Websites connected to Squarespace include SSL certificates that protect data transmitted between the website and its visitors. Squarespace also manages infrastructure, software updates, hosting performance, and platform security.

Annual website subscriptions include one year of free registration for an eligible new custom domain. Users can also connect or transfer a domain purchased from another provider.

## How to Create a Website with Squarespace

Creating a Squarespace website follows a guided setup process.

### Step 1: Start a Free Trial

Create a Squarespace account and begin the 14-day free trial. A credit card is not required to start testing the website builder.

### Step 2: Choose a Template or AI Builder

Select a professionally designed template or use Blueprint AI Builder to generate a personalized starting point.

Choose a design that matches the type of website being created, such as a portfolio, business site, blog, restaurant page, or online store.

### Step 3: Create the Website Structure

Add the main pages needed for the website. Common pages include:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Portfolio
- Blog
- Store
- Contact

Arrange the pages within the navigation menu and create dropdown menus when necessary.

### Step 4: Customize the Design

Use Fluid Engine to change page layouts, typography, colors, images, buttons, spacing, and other visual elements.

Review global design settings to ensure that the website maintains a consistent appearance across every page.

### Step 5: Add Content and Business Tools

Add text, images, galleries, videos, contact forms, products, newsletter forms, booking links, or membership content.

Review every page for accuracy, clear calls to action, and mobile usability.

### Step 6: Connect a Domain

Register a new domain through Squarespace or connect an existing domain from another provider.

Eligible annual website plans include one year of free registration for a new custom domain.

### Step 7: Select a Plan and Publish

Choose the plan that matches the website's design, collaboration, selling, and marketing needs.

Preview the site on desktop and mobile devices before publishing it publicly.

## Adding Multimedia

Squarespace supports images, videos, audio, image galleries, slideshows, background media, and downloadable files.

Users can upload their own images or add visual content through supported integrations. Built-in image tools help crop, resize, format, and display images across different layouts.

Video blocks allow users to embed or upload video content. Businesses can use videos for product demonstrations, brand introductions, testimonials, tutorials, or portfolio presentations.

Galleries are particularly useful for photographers, designers, restaurants, real estate professionals, and ecommerce stores. Responsive layouts help media display correctly on different screen sizes.

## Marketing Features

Squarespace includes several tools that help businesses attract visitors and communicate with customers.

Users can add newsletter forms, promotional pop-ups, announcement bars, contact forms, social media links, and customer profiles. The Contacts panel keeps subscribers, customers, members, donors, and leads organized in one area.

Squarespace Email Campaigns allows businesses to create newsletters and automated emails using designs that match their website. However, Email Campaigns is generally a separate subscription rather than a standard inclusion with every website plan.

The platform also supports integrations with services such as Mailchimp, Zapier, OpenTable, Google Analytics, and other business tools.

## SEO Features and Other Add-Ons

Squarespace includes built-in SEO features across its website plans. Users can customize page titles, meta descriptions, URLs, image alt text, social sharing images, and other search-related settings.

The platform automatically generates technical elements such as sitemaps and mobile-friendly page structures. It also includes AI-powered SEO guidance and scanning tools that can recommend improvements to titles, descriptions, images, and page content.

Website owners can connect Google Search Console to review indexing and search performance. Clear headings, useful content, internal links, relevant keywords, and optimized images remain important for long-term SEO performance.

Squarespace also offers separate or additional services, including Acuity Scheduling, Google Workspace, Email Campaigns, Bio Sites, and Unfold. These services may require their own subscriptions.

## Ecommerce

Squarespace allows users to sell physical products, digital products, services, memberships, courses, subscriptions, and gift cards. All current paid website plans include selling capabilities, although transaction fees and advanced commerce tools vary by plan.

Store owners can add product images, descriptions, variants, pricing, inventory quantities, categories, and shipping information. The dashboard also supports order management, discounts, customer accounts, sales analytics, and promotional tools.

Higher plans add features such as abandoned-cart recovery, advanced merchandising, subscription products, carrier-calculated shipping, commerce APIs, and lower payment processing rates.

Squarespace is suitable for small and medium-sized stores that prioritize design and content. Businesses with highly complex inventory, international operations, or extensive app requirements may prefer a dedicated ecommerce platform.

## Payments

Squarespace supports its integrated Squarespace Payments system, along with other compatible payment options depending on the business location.

Squarespace Payments can accept major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, ACH, Klarna, Afterpay, and other supported methods. Available payment options vary by country.

For standard domestic cards in the United States, current Squarespace Payments processing rates are approximately:

| Plan | Standard Domestic Card Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| Core | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| Plus | 2.7% + $0.30 |
| Advanced | 2.5% + $0.30 |

The Basic plan also carries a 2% Squarespace online-store transaction fee, while Core, Plus, and Advanced list a 0% online-store transaction fee. Payment-processing charges still apply separately.

## Squarespace Pricing

Squarespace currently offers four website plans: Basic, Core, Plus, and Advanced. The company recently raised several U.S. annual plan prices, so users should verify the final amount shown during checkout.

| Plan | Approximate Monthly Cost With Annual Billing | Main Features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $19 per month | Templates, drag-and-drop editor, hosting, SSL, SEO tools, basic analytics, two contributors, and selling tools |
| Core | $29 per month | Unlimited contributors, advanced customization, premium integrations, stronger analytics, and no Squarespace store transaction fee |
| Plus | $49 per month | Lower payment rates, advanced ecommerce tools, stronger digital-selling features, and expanded video storage |
| Advanced | $99 per month | Lowest standard payment rates, advanced selling and shipping tools, commerce APIs, and the highest usage limits |

These prices reflect approximate U.S. costs when paid annually following the July 2026 pricing update. Monthly billing costs more, and prices may differ by location, taxes, promotions, and account eligibility.

Squarespace does not offer a permanent free plan. However, users can test the platform through a 14-day free trial before selecting a paid subscription. Annual plans cost less per month than monthly subscriptions and include one year of eligible custom-domain registration for a new website.

## Customer Support

Squarespace offers customer support through email and live chat. Email support is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, while live chat is generally available during most weekdays. Squarespace does not provide standard phone support.

The platform also provides a detailed Help Center with written guides, videos, troubleshooting instructions, and website-building tutorials.

Users can attend free webinars covering website setup, page creation, styling, domains, and other platform features. Community forums allow customers to ask questions and exchange advice with other Squarespace users and professionals.

Businesses that need professional design or development assistance can also hire an independent Squarespace Expert through the platform's expert marketplace.

## Conclusion

Squarespace provides a polished and reliable website builder for businesses, creators, bloggers, and online sellers. The platform combines professional templates, visual editing, hosting, security, ecommerce, analytics, and marketing tools within one system.

Its greatest strength is design. Users can create visually impressive websites without coding while maintaining consistent typography, spacing, imagery, and mobile presentation.

Squarespace also provides enough business tools to support service websites, portfolios, blogs, membership programs, and small-to-medium online stores. Built-in SEO settings, analytics, contact management, and selling tools help businesses manage more of their online activity from one dashboard.

The main disadvantages are the lack of a permanent free plan and the higher cost of advanced features. Some users may also find dedicated ecommerce platforms more suitable for large or highly complex online stores.

Overall, Squarespace is a strong option for users who want an all-in-one website builder with professional design quality and integrated business tools. It is particularly well suited to creative professionals, service businesses, personal brands, restaurants, consultants, and small businesses that want a polished online presence without managing technical hosting systems.
