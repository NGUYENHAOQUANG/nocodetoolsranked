## About Hostinger Website Builder

Hostinger started as a web hosting company and later expanded into website building tools. The brand focuses on fast hosting, affordable plans, and beginner friendly products. Many small businesses and creators use Hostinger for launching websites without coding knowledge.

The company began operations in 2004 and now serves millions of users across many countries. Over time, Hostinger introduced its own website builder to help people create sites quickly. The builder focuses on simplicity, speed, and modern design templates.

Hostinger Website Builder combines hosting, domain tools, and design features into one platform. Users can build a site using drag editing tools or AI powered setup. The platform aims to reduce the steps needed to launch a website.

Another reason people choose Hostinger is pricing. The builder costs less than many competing platforms. At the same time, it offers features like online stores, SEO tools, and marketing options.

This review covers how the builder works, its features, pricing, and who should consider using it.

## How it Works?

Hostinger Website Builder focuses on quick website creation without coding. The platform combines AI tools, drag editing, and ready templates. This approach helps beginners launch a website within a short time.

Users begin by choosing a template or using the AI builder. The AI system asks several questions about business type and design preferences. It then creates a basic website layout with pages and content sections.

Once the site structure appears, the user can edit it using drag and drop controls. Sections can move easily across the page. Text, images, and buttons can be changed with simple clicks.

The builder also connects with hosting services automatically. Users do not need separate hosting setup. Everything stays managed within one dashboard.

This process keeps website creation simple for new users. It also saves time for people launching their first website.
